About YEGToday

Who we are, how we report, and the parts of the site that are still being built.

About us

YEGToday is a local news site for Edmonton and the communities around it, from St. Albert and Sherwood Park to Leduc, Spruce Grove, Fort Saskatchewan and Enoch Cree Nation.

We bring the region's news together in one place: short summaries of what other outlets are reporting, each linking to the original, plus our own reporting.

How we report

Most stories are summaries, in our own words, of reporting by other news outlets. Each one names the outlet and links to its story, so you can read the full version.

When YEGToday reports a story itself, we say who told us each fact, such as police at the scene, and we list what we don't know yet.

Photos come from freely licensed sources such as Wikimedia Commons and Flickr, and every one is credited at the bottom of the page it appears on.

Corrections

If we get something wrong, we fix it and note the change at the bottom of the story.

Ethics

We don't copy other outlets' stories or photos. We credit the people and outlets whose work we draw on, and we keep identifiable private people out of photos in crime and controversy stories.

Privacy & cookies

YEGToday doesn't use reader accounts, cookies, analytics or ad trackers.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and its fonts load from Google Fonts, so, as with any website, those services can see your IP address when you visit.

Terms of use

Our summaries link to the original reporting, which belongs to the outlets that published it. Photos are used under the licences listed in each page's photo credits. Full terms of use are coming soon.

Accessibility

We build YEGToday to work with a keyboard and with screen readers, with readable text contrast, and with less motion if your device asks for it. If something doesn't work for you, we want to hear about it once our contact address is up.

Coming soon

Some parts of YEGToday aren't ready yet: reader accounts and comments, newsletters, site search, audio versions of stories, live weather, poll voting and the poll archive, videos and podcasts, classifieds, the local business directory, gas prices and community discussions.

Until they're ready, the weather and poll results on our pages are samples, and the newsletter buttons don't sign you up for anything.
