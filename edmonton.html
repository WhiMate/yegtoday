Porsche driver, 23, was going 228 km/h, police at crash scene near Rogers Place say

No one was hurt in the single-vehicle crash, which left the front of a grey Porsche Taycan smashed in downtown Edmonton.

George Howell · YEGToday · Nov. 21

Downtown's City Centre mall sold for $57.3M, with 1,500 homes planned over the old Bay store

Edmonton developer Westrich Pacific has bought Edmonton City Centre and plans a mixed-use complex with housing over the former Hudson's Bay space, plus new stores, entertainment and a Nordic spa.

CTV News Edmonton · Nov. 20

Kehewin Cree Nation proposes Edmonton's first urban reserve on Blatchford land

The First Nation wants to buy 14 hectares of the former airport lands for $1 for housing, health and education services, a spa and a 180-room hotel. Council's executive committee takes it up next week.

CTV News Edmonton · Nov. 20

Province says City of Champions signs will return to highways into Edmonton

Transportation Minister Devin Dreeshen says Alberta will install new welcome signs with the slogan to mark the Elks' Grey Cup win. The city's old welcome signs carrying it came down in 2015.

CBC Edmonton · Nov. 20

Three downtown parkades drop to a $5 flat rate for December

All of December, EPark will charge a $5 flat rate on weekends and on weekday evenings from 5 p.m. at the Canada Place, City Hall and Stanley A. Milner parkades to draw holiday crowds downtown.

CTV News Edmonton · Nov. 20

Oil Kings trade for St. Albert-born winger Ty Meunier to boost their scoring

The WHL club sent two conditional 2030 bantam draft picks to Swift Current for the 18-year-old forward. He was the seventh overall bantam pick and is now on his fourth junior team.

Edmonton Journal · Nov. 20

Police urge Edmonton-area drivers to ease off as snow slicks the roads

Edmonton-area police issued a winter driving reminder on Friday as falling snow left roads slick around the capital region.

CTV News Edmonton · Nov. 20

Judge's order empties troubled Stadium Plaza apartments in Edmonton's Parkdale

Tenants of the 78-unit building on 86 Street had until noon Friday to leave; court records cite squatters, drug and gang activity, and copper thefts that knocked out the heat.

Global News · Nov. 20

Lightning snap McDavid's 23-game season-opening point streak with 3-1 win

The run matched Wayne Gretzky's 1988-89 streak for fourth-longest from opening night in NHL history. Colton Dach scored Edmonton's lone goal as the Oilers fell to 17-4-3.

NHL.com · Nov. 20

Oilers tribute built from empty pop cases towers nearly 17 feet in a Mayfield grocery store

Eric Falkenberg, who builds pop-case displays at a Kelowna Save-On-Foods, prepped the McDavid and Draisaitl piece in B.C. over about three weeks before assembling it at the Mayfield store, which reports extra foot traffic.

CTV News Edmonton · Nov. 20

Seniors and toddlers bond over songs and stories at Canterbury Foundation literacy program

Every Friday, residents join babies, toddlers and parents for the Centre for Family Literacy's Do-Re-Mi, 123 program. It began three years ago with about five families and now often draws 50-plus residents.

CTV News Edmonton · Nov. 20

Gerein: Edmonton earned the champions label again, but it shouldn't be the city's brand

The Journal columnist welcomes the civic pride around the Elks but calls the province's new City of Champions highway signs a political stunt. He argues Edmonton needs a fresher, more distinctive slogan of its own.

Keith Gerein · Edmonton Journal via Yahoo · Nov. 20

Gunter: Province's bike lane bill is common sense, and a city memo shows why

The columnist backs the UCP bill letting the province review and remove bike lanes. He argues a city memo pricing removal at $150 million to $170 million concedes that the lanes affected vehicle travel.

Lorne Gunter · Edmonton Journal via Yahoo · Nov. 20

Alberta bill would let the province remove city bike lanes, and Edmonton is pushing back

Bill 43 would ban new bike lanes that restrict car traffic, let the province remove some existing ones at city expense and add provincial oversight of LRT plans. Mayor Andrew Knack called it dangerous.

CBC Edmonton · Nov. 19

Crime-mapping software behind drop in violent crime, EPS tells police commission

The CompStat system, piloted in northwest Edmonton and used citywide since February, flags hot spots and repeat offenders early; violent crime is down nearly 11 per cent so far this year, the commission heard.

Edmonton Journal via Yahoo · Nov. 19

Edmonton's fast-growing AI, defence and life sciences sectors remain a small slice of jobs

Jobs in those clusters rose 21% from 2019 to 2025 and pay about $27,000 above the regional average, but they make up just 2% of employment, the Edmonton Chamber of Commerce reports.

Taproot Edmonton · Nov. 19

Eighteen Edmonton artists each receive $15,000 from the Edmonton Artists' Trust Fund

Recipients announced Nov. 16 include photographer Jordon Hon, muralist Busyrawk and drummer Biboye Onanuga. The Edmonton Arts Council says its city funding has stayed flat since 2019.

Taproot Edmonton · Nov. 19

Police chief says cybercrime and AI are outpacing EPS without more capital funding

Chief Warren Driechel told CBC's Edmonton AM the funding formula nets only about 53 new staff, 22 of them officers, and that one phase of an extortion probe cost an extra $1 million.

CBC Edmonton via Yahoo · Nov. 18

Costco trades its long-running 50 Street store for a bigger warehouse in northeast Edmonton

The 160,000-plus-square-foot store at 285 Ebbers Boulevard NW opened Nov. 18 with a 24-pump gas bar, a liquor store and over 1,000 parking stalls, adding more than 80 jobs.

InvestorsHub · Nov. 18

Hawrelak Park adds ice bumper cars, ice bikes and skate rentals this winter

Hawrelak Recreation Co., the park's equipment rental company, announced a new cold-weather lineup that also includes skate sharpening. It will be the park's first winter open to the public after its years-long closure.

Daily Hive · Nov. 18

Government Centre LRT station is set to become Buffalo Crossing

A city naming committee voted 4-0 for the name, which recalls a historic buffalo crossing on the river. Council is expected to sign off Nov. 25, and new station artwork should be finished by the end of 2027.

Edmonton Journal · Nov. 17

Lower speed limits on parts of Jasper Avenue and Gateway Boulevard move a step closer

Edmonton's urban planning committee backed cutting limits from 50 to 40 km/h on busy roads in or near business districts, including parts of 107 Avenue and 124 Street. City council still has to approve it.

CTV News Edmonton · Nov. 17

Elks beat Alouettes 37-30 to win the 113th Grey Cup, their first title since 2015

Cody Fajardo passed for two touchdowns and rushed for two more, taking MVP honours at Calgary's McMahon Stadium as Edmonton won its 15th Grey Cup a year after placing last in the West.

TSN · Nov. 15

Late goal gives Golden Bears men's soccer a U SPORTS bronze over top-seeded UBC

Down a goal in Victoria, the University of Alberta tied it in the 68th minute and won 2-1 on Tyson Blight's 87th-minute strike, the program's first national medal since 2016.

Golden Bears & Pandas · Nov. 15

Jasper Avenue's Coffee Bureau nominated for Canada's Favourite Business 2026

Husband-and-wife owners Peter West and Christane Tassinari opened the café in 2015 and added a Wîhkwêntôwin location in 2024. A customer's nomination puts the shop in the running for a $1,000 gift card.

CTV News Edmonton · Nov. 15

Two Edmonton schools tie for second in Fraser Institute's Alberta rankings

Mount Pleasant and Windsor Park tied for second among 699 Alberta schools, but four of the 10 lowest-ranked were also in Edmonton. A U of A education instructor calls test scores alone too narrow a measure.

CTV News Edmonton · Nov. 12

Edmonton chocolate maker and Strathcona County pierogi farm join the global ÉCONOMUSÉE network

Antoine Lavoie's Delavoye Chocolate Maker and Agnes Kulinska's Pierogerie Polish Foods became the Edmonton region's first members of the artisan network. Both are building visitor experiences around their craft.

Taproot Edmonton · Nov. 12

Urgent care centres are coming, but south Edmonton still wants its hospital

Alberta set aside $39 million to buy land and plan nine urgent care centres, two in Edmonton. Councillors say the south side still needs a hospital, and the Heritage Valley hospital site sits empty.

CTV News Edmonton · Nov. 11

Slain officers' families address court at sentencing of man who sold the rifle

Prosecutors want 12 to 14 years and the defence 4½ for the man found guilty of manslaughter for selling the rifle used to kill constables Travis Jordan and Brett Ryan in March 2023.

Narcity (The Canadian Press) · Nov. 10

Green Onion Cake Man to leave its Alberta Avenue storefront when the lease ends in April 2027

The shop at 9132 118th Ave. NW says its lease runs out in April 2027. It's unclear whether a new spot is planned, but the cakes are still sold at stores including H&W Produce and the Italian Centre Shop.

Daily Hive · Nov. 10

New Edmonton building opens 40 supportive homes for homeless veterans

Veterans' House Canada opened its second supportive housing site, a roughly $15-million building at 13015 128 Ave. NW. It has on-site staff, a library, a garden and a park for service pets.

CTV News Edmonton · Nov. 9

Duchess Bake Shop scraps Harry Potter gingerbread theme in favour of a holiday village

After criticism linked to J.K. Rowling's views on transgender people, Duchess is building a gingerbread holiday village instead, with donations going to the Pride Centre of Edmonton and matched by the bakery.

CTV News Edmonton · Nov. 8

Retired RCMP vehicles slated for scrap were resold instead, Alberta Mounties allege

Five men face charges after an RCMP auto theft unit probe; police allege a worker at a northwest Edmonton recycler faked destruction records, and tie the ring to 80-plus other stolen vehicles.

Global News · Nov. 4

Edmonton police say draft city budget leaves $61.6M hole in technology funding

EPS asked for nearly $183 million in capital spending through 2030 and stands to get roughly two-thirds; the chief says equipping a patrol vehicle now costs more than $100,000, up from $65,000.

Global News · Nov. 4

Junior mixed doubles curling worlds return to Edmonton's Saville Centre for a third year

The open-entry World Curling event runs May 3-9 at the Saville Community Sports Centre, which also hosted this year's edition, when Japan edged Canada 6-5 in the final.

TSN · Nov. 3

City proposes 4.9% property tax hike for 2027 in its new four-year budget

The draft 2027-30 plan would add about $200 to the average homeowner's bill. It includes a $6.3-billion capital budget with $100 million to widen Parsons Road, 215 Street and 41 Avenue.

CTV News Edmonton · Nov. 2

Edmonton Valley Zoo builds its first Day of the Dead altar to honour animals it has lost

Created with the Mexican Society of Edmonton, the ofrenda remembers more than 100 zoo animals that have died since 1959, including Cactus, a 27-year-old porcupine. Visitors were invited to add photos of their pets.

CTV News Edmonton · Nov. 1

High Level Bridge to close for most of 2027 for urgent storm repairs

Landslides and erosion from 2026's record summer rain mean the 113-year-old bridge will close from spring to late fall 2027. Its planned rehabilitation will happen at the same time. Detours are not yet set.

Global News · Oct. 30

Nov. 10, 1974: Edmonton's new Coliseum opens with an Oilers win over Cleveland

The $17.3-million arena, built to replace the aging Edmonton Gardens, opened with a WHA game the Oilers won 4-1 over the Cleveland Crusaders. Strikes slowed construction, and the last 5,000 seats arrived just before opening night.

Nov. 27, 1954: Jackie Parker's fumble return delivers Edmonton's first Grey Cup

Parker picked up a Montreal fumble and ran about 90 yards to tie the game. Bob Dean's convert then sealed a 26-25 upset of the favoured Alouettes in Toronto.
