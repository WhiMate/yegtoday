Province says City of Champions signs will return to highways into Edmonton

Alberta's transportation minister says new welcome signs carrying the old slogan will mark the Edmonton Elks' Grey Cup win. The city took its own City of Champions signs down in 2015.

YEGToday staff Nov. 20, 2026 · Summary of reporting by CBC News

Read the original at CBC News >
Listen to this article00:02:10

EDMONTON — Drivers heading into Edmonton will soon be greeted by "City of Champions" signs again. Alberta's transportation minister plans to put the slogan on new provincial welcome signs to mark the Edmonton Elks' Grey Cup win, CBC News reported Friday.

Transportation Minister Devin Dreeshen unveiled the signs at the legislature, where he had invited members of the team, and also announced the plan on X on Thursday afternoon. He told CBC's Radio Active that the signs will stand at the entrances to the city, welcoming visitors and noting that the Elks brought the Grey Cup back to Alberta.

The idea came from quarterback Cody Fajardo, who suggested bringing the signs back after Edmonton beat the Montreal Alouettes 37-30 in the 113th Grey Cup on Sunday, Nov. 15. It was the club's first championship since 2015.

Still unanswered

  • What the signs will cost. CBC asked the minister's office and had no answer before publication.
  • Whether the province talked to city officials first. That was also asked, with no reply yet.
  • Exactly where and when the signs go up. Only "the entrances of the city" has been mentioned.

Where the slogan came from

The tagline first went on the city's own welcome signs in 1988. It honoured both the way residents came together after the deadly 1987 tornado and Edmonton's run of sports titles. The city removed those signs in 2015.

Earlier in the week, Mayor Andrew Knack was asked whether the city would bring its signs back. He wouldn't give a yes or no, and said he was worried the debate would pull attention away from the Elks' win, according to CBC.

CBC says it also contacted the City of Edmonton and the Elks for comment and had not heard back by the time the story was posted.
